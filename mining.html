Who We Are

Ghana's Emerging Leader in Mobile On-Site Refueling

EMEX-PL Ghana Limited is a privately owned, limited liability Oil Marketing Company incorporated in August 2017 (Reg. No. CS225942017). We commenced operations in September 2017 under Ghana's Companies Code, 1963 (Act 179).

We understand that fuel can account for up to 40% of operational costs. That's why our mission is not simply to supply petroleum products — but to become a strategic partner that frees our clients from non-core operations, letting them focus on what they do best.

With strategic partnerships with mining companies like Rocksure International and Thonket Plantpool Limited, we deliver fuel 24/7 directly to client equipment, billed per delivered quantity — no overstocking, no pilfering, no wasted budget.

What We Do

Services Offered

End-to-end petroleum solutions engineered for the industries that keep Ghana moving.

Bulk Fuel Supply

We deliver all grades of gasoline and diesel from major refineries in Kumasi, Takoradi, and Tema. Serving gas stations, mining, telecoms, and large fleet operators with 200,000–460,000 litres/month.

  • Top-tier delivery fleet
  • Direct refinery sourcing
  • Competitive pricing plans

On-Site Refueling

Let us manage your refueling operations end-to-end. We deploy commercial-grade tanks, pumps, filters, nozzles and dedicated staff to deliver fuel directly into your equipment 24/7.

  • 24/7 shift system
  • Zero pilfering risk
  • Pay per delivered quantity

Bulk Lubricant Supply

High-grade mining and industrial lubricants stored and supplied at competitive rates. We carry Castrol, Ecolubrium, Caltex, and Texaco — all rigorously tested before and after use.

  • Castrol / Caltex / Texaco
  • Heat-tolerant mining grades
  • Below-market pricing
